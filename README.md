## 📈 Indicators Implemented

- **SMA** – Smooths price data for trend detection and signal generation
- **EMA** – Reacts faster to recent price changes; ideal for short-term strategies
- **WMA** – Weighted emphasis on recent prices; useful for swing trading
- **MACD** – Combines fast and slow EMAs to detect momentum shifts
- **Chaikin Volatility** – Measures percentage change in EMA of high-low range
- **ATR** – Gauges market volatility for dynamic stop-loss placement
